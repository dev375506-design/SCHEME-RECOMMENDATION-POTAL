# 🌱 Udyam Setu

> **Connecting local entrepreneurs to customers, opportunities, and government support.**

**SIH 2026 · Problem Statement 92 · MVP**

Udyam Setu is a digital platform designed to help **marginalised and local entrepreneurs become discoverable, connect with nearby customers, and access relevant government schemes** through a single portal.

---

## ✨ What It Does

- 📍 **Geo-Located Discovery** — Find entrepreneurs and businesses near you.
- 🗺️ **Interactive Map** — Explore local businesses through map-based discovery.
- 👤 **Entrepreneur Profiles** — Showcase business details, location, category, and contact information.
- 📝 **Business Registration** — Entrepreneurs can register and create their business profile.
- 🏛️ **Scheme Discovery** — Connect entrepreneurs with relevant government schemes.
- ✅ **Verification System** — Admins can review and verify registered entrepreneurs.
- 🔎 **Search & Filtering** — Discover businesses by category, location, and other criteria.
- 📊 **Entrepreneur Dashboard** — Manage profiles and view relevant opportunities.

---

## 🔄 How It Works

```text
Entrepreneur
     ↓
Register Business
     ↓
Location + Business Details
     ↓
Admin Verification
     ↓
┌──────────────────────┐
│ Udyam Setu Platform  │
└──────────────────────┘
     ↓
Nearby Customers ←→ Local Entrepreneurs
     ↓
Relevant Government Schemes
````

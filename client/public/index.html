A digital bridge for marginalised & local entrepreneurs

Millions of small, home-based and marginalised entrepreneurs remain invisible to customers, government schemes and support services — simply because they aren't discoverable online. Udyam Setu helps them register, get found nearby, and connect to the right support.

Find Local Entrepreneurs Register Your Business

The problem we're solving

Artisans, home-based cooks, tailors, small farmers and local service providers often run viable businesses with no online presence. They struggle to reach customers beyond their immediate neighbourhood, don't know which government schemes they're eligible for, and have no simple way to be discovered by people actively looking for what they sell.

What the portal does

  • ● Lets entrepreneurs register with location, category and a business profile.
  • ● Makes them discoverable by category, location and a live map.
  • ● Matches them to relevant government schemes automatically.
  • ● Gives admins a simple way to verify genuine businesses.

Key features

📍

Location-first discovery

GPS-based registration and an interactive map so customers can find entrepreneurs nearby, not just by keyword.

🔎

Searchable directory

Filter by category, state, district or product/service, with a clean profile page for every business.

🏛️

Government scheme matching

Entrepreneurs see schemes relevant to their category — eligibility, benefits and official links in one place.

✅

Verified profiles

A lightweight admin review flow builds trust between customers and first-time online entrepreneurs.

📱

Direct contact

Call or WhatsApp an entrepreneur directly from their profile — no middleman required.

📊

Simple dashboards

Entrepreneurs manage their own profile; admins track coverage and verification at a glance.
